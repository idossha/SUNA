Galaxies falling into dense cluster environments experience ram pressure
from the intracluster medium [@gunn1972], a process now observed in rich
detail across the nearby universe [@cortese2021; @boselli2022]. Molecular
gas has been found surviving deep into stripped tails [@jachym2019],
raising the question of how quickly star formation actually shuts down.

The classical stripping condition compares ram pressure with the disk's
restoring force per unit area:

$$ {#eq:stripping}
P_\mathrm{ram} = \rho_\mathrm{ICM} v^2 > 2\pi G \Sigma_\ast \Sigma_\mathrm{gas}
$$

When the inequality in @eq:stripping holds at radius $r$, gas outside $r$
is removed on roughly a crossing time. In this demo manuscript we
illustrate the SUNA workflow end to end: the spectrum and scaling relation
in @fig:fig-spectrum and the velocity field in @fig:fig-velocity-map are
each generated by a script stored beside the figure, so every panel is
reproducible from the data in this project.
