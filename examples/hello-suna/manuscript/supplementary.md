# Supplementary Methods

The weekly timesheet behind @fig:timesheet was kept by hand, in a text file, by
one person who knew what the study was about. It is reproduced from
`data/timesheet.csv` by `figures/timesheet/source/plot.py` with matplotlib
[@hunter2007]; the parameters below reproduce the figure exactly.
